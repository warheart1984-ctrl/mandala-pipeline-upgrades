## The door that remembers

The archive does not open for the curious. It opens for those who already know what they will lose.

## Consent as weight

Ink on the ledger is heavier than stone. Each name is a vow that outlives the hand that wrote it.

## The witness

They stand in the amber corridor and refuse to invent a kinder history. The walls listen. The dust does not.

## First refusal

A request arrives disguised as mercy. The witness answers with the only honest word left: no.

## The courtyard beyond paper

Outside, rain finds the courtyard stones. Inside, the record holds. Between them, a thin door of law.
